# Nexora AI account and role model

- Every public registration creates a student account.
- A verified student may apply to become a peer tutor from the Peer Tutors page.
- Peer-tutor capabilities are tied to an owned `PeerTutor` profile and server-side ownership checks; they are not granted through public role selection.
- Administrator accounts must be provisioned by a trusted operator. They are never offered through public registration, demo controls, or shared credentials.
- Accessibility support presets are preferences, not medical diagnoses. No diagnosis is required.

This model should be reviewed before introducing institution-level educator/class-management features. Do not add an “Educator” registration choice until its permissions and approval workflow are defined server-side.
